🌸 He reached for the sky — she held him so he'd never fall 🌸

There is a photograph taken long ago — a little boy in a green-striped sweater, arm stretched wide toward a branch heavy with red apples, his face lit up with the kind of pure, uncontained joy that only children know. He did not realize then what he knows now: that the greatest gift life would ever offer him was already right there beside him — his mother's love.

She watched him that day with eyes that held entire universes. She let him reach, let him explore, let him believe he could touch the sky — because that is what mothers do. They hold you up so you can stretch further than you ever thought possible.

And today, dressed in the softest lavender, her hands adorned with henna's intricate patterns, she still holds him — not always in her arms, but in every warm glance, every quiet prayer whispered at dawn, every meal made with love, every worry carried silently so he need not carry it himself.

He was always in her hands. He always will be. That little boy reaching for an apple — he is still reaching for the sky. And she is still there, making sure he can.
